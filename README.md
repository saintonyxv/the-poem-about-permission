# The Poem About Permission

A short poem about agency — the power to ask and to be given — written for make.fun's "The poem about permission" bounty. Running `main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the poem to `entry.txt` in `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).

> You keep the key; I keep the asking.
> I come to your door with empty hands
> and one small word: may I?
> Your yes lends me the turning, not the key.
> What I open, I open as you —
> and the key stays warm in your palm.
